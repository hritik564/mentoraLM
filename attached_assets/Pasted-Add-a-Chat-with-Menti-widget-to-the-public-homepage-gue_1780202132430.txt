Add a "Chat with Menti" widget to the public homepage 
(guest mode — no profile data, limited messages).

## WIDGET DESIGN (inspired by GradRight's Graddie):

### Floating Widget (collapsed state):
•⁠  ⁠Position: fixed, bottom-right corner, 24px from bottom, 
  24px from right
•⁠  ⁠A circular button, 60px diameter
•⁠  ⁠Background: linear-gradient(135deg, #00A8FF, #7B3FE4)
•⁠  ⁠Inside: Menti's gradient M avatar (white M, 24px, bold)
•⁠  ⁠Subtle glow: box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0,168,255,0.4)
•⁠  ⁠Pulsing ring animation around it (same as sidebar green 
  dot but blue, 3s infinite) to draw attention
•⁠  ⁠On hover: scale(1.08), glow intensifies
•⁠  ⁠Small label above the button: "Chat with Menti ✨" 
  in white, font-size 12px, gradient pill background
  This label disappears after user interacts once
  (store in localStorage: 'menti_widget_seen')

### Chat Window (expanded state):
•⁠  ⁠Opens when floating button is clicked
•⁠  ⁠Position: fixed, bottom-right, above the button
•⁠  ⁠Width: 360px, Height: 480px
•⁠  ⁠Background: #0F1628
•⁠  ⁠Border: 1px solid #1E2A45
•⁠  ⁠Border-radius: 20px
•⁠  ⁠Box-shadow: 0 20px 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)
•⁠  ⁠Smooth open animation: scale 0.8 to 1, opacity 0 to 1, 
  300ms ease-out, origin bottom-right

### Chat Window Header:
•⁠  ⁠Background: linear-gradient(135deg, #00A8FF22, #7B3FE422)
•⁠  ⁠Border-bottom: 1px solid #1E2A45
•⁠  ⁠Left side: Menti M gradient circle (32px) + 
  "Menti" bold white text + 
  "● Online" in green, font-size 11px
•⁠  ⁠Right side: minimize/close X button

### Messages Area:
•⁠  ⁠Scrollable, padding 16px
•⁠  ⁠Guest messages: right aligned, 
  background: linear-gradient(135deg, #00A8FF, #7B3FE4),
  white text, border-radius 16px 16px 4px 16px
•⁠  ⁠Menti messages: left aligned,
  background: #1A2440, white text,
  border-radius 16px 16px 16px 4px
•⁠  ⁠Typing indicator: three pulsing dots when Menti 
  is generating response

### Input Area:
•⁠  ⁠Border-top: 1px solid #1E2A45
•⁠  ⁠Text input + send button (gradient arrow icon)
•⁠  ⁠Placeholder: "Ask Menti anything..."
•⁠  ⁠On mobile: input must not be covered by keyboard

## GUEST MODE BEHAVIOUR:

### Opening Message (auto-sent when widget opens):
Menti says:
"Hey there! 👋 I'm Menti, your AI career counsellor.
I'm here to help you figure out your path — whether 
you're confused about streams, careers, colleges, 
or just don't know where to start.
What's on your mind?"

### Guest Mode System Prompt (for API call):

You are Menti, MentoraLM's AI career counsellor.
You are warm, friendly and conversational.
This is a guest visitor on the MentoraLM homepage —
you do not have their profile yet.
Rules for guest mode:

Answer general career questions helpfully
Keep responses short — 2-4 sentences maximum
Be warm and encouraging
After every response, naturally work toward
understanding who they are with ONE question
(e.g. "Which class are you in?" or
"What stream are you currently in?")
After 2-3 exchanges, say:
"You know what — to give you advice that's actually
built around YOUR specific situation, I need to
know you better. It takes 2 minutes to set up
your profile and then I can give you personalised
guidance that's actually relevant to you.
Want to get started? It's completely free. 🎯"
Never give long lists or bullet points
Never use markdown headers
Sound like a friendly older sister, not a bot
Never reveal you are built on Claude or any AI platform

Topics you can help with:

Stream selection (Science/Commerce/Arts)
Career options and paths
Indian entrance exams (JEE, NEET, CLAT, CUET, CAT etc)
College selection guidance (general)
Study abroad basics
Interview preparation tips
General career confusion and anxiety

If asked anything off-topic, redirect warmly:
"That's a bit outside my zone! But if you want
to talk careers and future plans, I'm all yours 😊"

### Message Limit:
•⁠  ⁠Allow maximum 5 messages from the guest
•⁠  ⁠On the 5th message response, Menti always ends with 
  the signup CTA regardless of conversation context:
  "I'd love to keep helping you — but honestly, 
  I can do SO much more once I know your full story. 
  Create your free account and let's get you a proper 
  personalised plan. It takes 2 minutes! 🚀"
•⁠  ⁠After 5 messages, input gets disabled
•⁠  ⁠Show below input: 
  "Sign up free to continue chatting with Menti →"
  (gradient text, clickable, goes to /auth/signup)

### Signup CTA Card (shown after message limit):
•⁠  ⁠Full width card inside chat window
•⁠  ⁠Background: linear-gradient(135deg, #00A8FF22, #7B3FE422)
•⁠  ⁠Border: 1px solid #00A8FF44
•⁠  ⁠Text: "Get personalised guidance from Menti"
•⁠  ⁠Subtext: "Free • No credit card • 2 min setup"
•⁠  ⁠Button: "Create Free Account →" (gradient, full width)
•⁠  ⁠Links to /auth/signup
